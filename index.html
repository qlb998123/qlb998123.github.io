HELLO, I’M

Libo Qiao

Computational biology, multi-omics & scientific AI

I study how genetic variation shapes biological systems. My work brings together single-cell multi-omics, genomic models, and scientific AI agents to connect genetic evidence with biological understanding.

I am currently in joint training at Nankai University and Guangzhou National Laboratory, advised by Fulong Yu and Baofa Sun. Previously, I studied at the University of Science and Technology of China with Xiuwu Bian, investigating glioma stem cell biology through computational and experimental approaches.

My current projects focus on automated post-GWAS interpretation and the regulation of human hematopoiesis. I’m also interested in lab-in-the-loop systems that bring computation and experiments closer together.
